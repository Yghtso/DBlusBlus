# Chapter 40 — Observability and EXPLAIN Verification

## V40-A — BufferPool and page-I/O metric accounting

Compose the unnumbered **Disk tests** and **Buffer management verification** procedures
under Storage Verification with `ARCHITECTURE.md` §§7.4, 7.6.3–7.6.4, 7.8, 7.10,
7.12.4, and 40.1. Reuse their load-intent, waiter, transfer, validation, victim,
new-page, and copied-writeback barriers and fault injections; do not infer an owner event
from the Chapter-40 metric being tested. For each row, capture reported metrics before and
after a controlled interval in one identified owner/unit/scope/lifetime, and compare the
deltas with a separate BufferPool state/claim/pin/publication trace and DiskManager
full-transfer event ledger or equivalent test-only owner observations. Identify normal-fetch
completed-claim totals (`L`), resident hits (`H`), load-publication misses (`M`), completed
full-page physical reads (`P`), and completed full-page page-write transfers (`W`). Do not
assume process-start zero or require a public reset API; exclude unrelated owner activity
with barriers or account for it independently. Missing owner evidence, unavailable mandatory
metric evidence, an untriggered fault, or an unvisited publication boundary is
`NOT VERIFIED / TEST INFRASTRUCTURE INCOMPLETE`, not PASS. Owner state determines fetch success, page
validity, residency, and stable writeback; the metric is only a diagnostic report.

other metric families; their event, gauge, duration, overflow, and availability integration
requires separate procedures.

**Append reservation, authorization, and physical failure** and **Durable prefix, group

**Transaction identity, snapshot, and status verification**, **COMMIT Fault-Injection
Tests**, **ABORT Fault-Injection Tests**, and the specified V39 procedures below with
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §§9.14–9.15, 12.12–12.15, 15.5–15.6, 39.1.5–39.1.7, and 40.1/40.3.
This family compares event diagnostics with those owners; it does not repeat their WAL or
terminal-protocol proofs.

For each fixture, snapshot reported totals `D0`/`D1` in an identified owner, unit, and
process/database-instance or explicitly reset diagnostic scope; assert exact relevant
`D1-D0` against a separate identity-correlated owner event ledger. Isolate unrelated
append, flush, and transaction activity with the existing barriers or subtract only
independently observed events. The WAL byte oracle independently constructs valid record
spans including published padding and counts newly covered bytes in the valid logical
prefix; record-start `durable_lsn` subtraction, intended bytes, physical-write attempts,
and waiter count are not byte oracles. The WAL-flusher attempt ledger distinguishes one
durability attempt from its constituent segment syscalls, its success/failure, and the
distinct commit waiters satisfied by a successful advancement. Missing owner or mandatory
diagnostic evidence, an untriggered injection, unproven coalescing, or an unreached
publication boundary is `NOT VERIFIED / TEST INFRASTRUCTURE INCOMPLETE`, never PASS.
Metrics report, but cannot decide, WAL authority, terminal state, or client delivery.
